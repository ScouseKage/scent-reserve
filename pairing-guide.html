The Pairing Guide

Layering combinations from your collection
Apply the heavier or more base-heavy scent first. Let it dry for 2–5 minutes, then apply the second on top. One spray of each is enough to test — layering amplifies everything, so start lighter than you think you need.

157Terre d'Hermès

The fresh verbena and violet of Green Irish Tweed lifts the grapefruit and vetiver of Terre d'Hermès into something genuinely outdoorsy — damp woodland in early spring. One of the most natural-feeling combinations in the collection.

Apply 157 first · GIT after 2 mins Spring
223Tobacco Vanille
+
300Angels' Share
Creates → A fireplace in a bottle

Both share boozy, warming DNA. The vanilla sweetness of Tobacco Vanille fills in around Angels' Share's cognac and cinnamon, creating something richer and more complete than either alone. The best combination in your collection.

Equal application · chest and wrists Autumn · Winter
149Baccarat Rouge 540
+
346Lime Basil & Mandarin
Creates → Airy citrus with a sweet saffron glow

BR540's airy sweetness pairs with the citrus brightness of Lime Basil & Mandarin. The lime and mandarin give BR540 a fresher, more summery opening before it settles into its signature warmth. A good warm-weather twist on an otherwise year-round fragrance.

346 first · BR540 lightly on top Summer · Early Autumn
170Oud Saffron
+
412Tuscan Leather
Creates → Spiced leather oud — bold and commanding

The saffron amplifies Tuscan Leather's raspberry-leather accord, while the oud adds smoky depth that leather alone doesn't have. A statement pairing — not a subtle one. Strictly a cold evening, special occasion combination. Not for the office.

412 on skin · 170 very lightly on top Winter only · Evening only
494Lost Cherry
+
219Bitter Peach
Creates → Dark fruit cocktail — boozy and seductive

Cherry and peach share enough fruity DNA that they complement rather than compete. Lost Cherry's boozy, rosy depth anchors Bitter Peach's lighter rum-and-fruit accord. Warm, slightly intoxicating, and firmly evening-only.

494 first as base · 219 lightly on wrists Autumn · Winter · Date Night
120Eros
+
149Baccarat Rouge 540
Creates → Fresh-sweet with magnetic sillage

Eros's fresh minty-green opening softens BR540's sweetness and gives it a more masculine edge, while BR540 adds warmth and longevity to Eros's sometimes-fleeting drydown. A genuinely crowd-pleasing combination with excellent projection.

BR540 on chest · Eros on neck and wrists Spring · Autumn
Pairing to Avoid
175Ombre Nomade
+
514Black Orchid
Creates → Suffocating — too much of everything

Both are among the heaviest fragrances in your collection. Layering two high-intensity resinous ouds produces something overwhelming rather than complex. These are better left to compete on separate evenings.
