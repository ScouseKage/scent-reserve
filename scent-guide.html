Layering fragrances means applying one first (usually the heavier or more base-heavy scent), letting it dry, then applying the second on top. Start light — one spray each is enough to test. The pairings below are chosen from your existing 15.

Recommended Pairings
318 Green Irish Tweed + 157 Terre d'Hermès
CREATES → Earthy fern with mineral depth

The fresh verbena and violet of Green Irish Tweed lifts the grapefruit and vetiver of Terre d'Hermès into something that smells genuinely outdoorsy — like walking through damp woodland in early spring. One of the most natural-feeling combinations in your collection.

Technique: Apply Terre d'Hermès first as base · GIT on pulse points after 2 mins
223 Tobacco Vanille + 300 Angels' Share
CREATES → Warm cognac tobacco — a fireplace in a bottle

Both fragrances share boozy, warming DNA. Together the vanilla sweetness of Tobacco Vanille fills in the gaps around Angels' Share's cognac and cinnamon, creating something richer and more complete than either alone. An absolute winter evening combination.

Technique: Equal application · both on chest and wrists · allow to blend on skin
149 Baccarat Rouge 540 + 346 Lime Basil & Mandarin
CREATES → Airy citrus with a sweet saffron glow

BR540's airy sweetness pairs surprisingly well with the citrus brightness of Lime Basil & Mandarin. The lime and mandarin give BR540 a fresher, more summery opening before it settles into its signature warmth. A good warm-weather twist on an otherwise year-round fragrance.

Technique: Lime Basil first · BR540 lightly on top · one spray each maximum
170 Oud Saffron + 412 Tuscan Leather
CREATES → Spiced leather oud — bold and commanding

This is a statement pairing, not a subtle one. The saffron in Oud Saffron amplifies Tuscan Leather's raspberry-leather accord, while the oud adds a smoky depth that leather alone doesn't have. Strictly a cold evening, special occasion combination — do not wear this to the office.

Technique: Tuscan Leather on skin first · Oud Saffron very lightly on top · one spray only
494 Lost Cherry + 219 Bitter Peach
CREATES → Dark fruit cocktail — boozy and seductive

Cherry and peach share enough fruity DNA that they work together rather than compete. Lost Cherry's boozy, rosy depth anchors Bitter Peach's lighter rum-and-fruit accord. The result is warm, slightly intoxicating, and distinctly evening-only. Good for a night out with Antonia.

Technique: Lost Cherry first as base · Bitter Peach lightly on wrists · let them marry for 5 mins
120 Eros + 149 Baccarat Rouge 540
CREATES → Fresh-sweet with magnetic sillage

Eros's fresh minty-green opening softens BR540's sweetness and gives it a more masculine edge, while BR540 adds warmth and longevity to Eros's sometimes-fleeting drydown. A genuinely crowd-pleasing combination with excellent projection for the temperature range where both work well.

Technique: BR540 on chest · Eros on neck and wrists · spring and autumn only
Pairings to Avoid
175 Ombre Nomade + 514 Black Orchid
CREATES → Suffocating — too much of everything

Both are among the heaviest fragrances in your collection. Layering two high-intensity, resinous ouds produces something overwhelming rather than complex. These are better left to compete on separate evenings.
