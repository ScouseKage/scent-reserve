Notes
  • Temperature matters as much as the calendar. A cold June day calls for autumn picks; a warm October calls for spring ones.
  • Heavy ouds (175, 170) amplify indoors with central heating — go lighter on application in winter.
  • 318 (Green Irish Tweed) and 149 (Baccarat Rouge 540) are your two genuine year-rounders. When in doubt, reach for one of these.
  • Your summer range is thin — only three fragrances genuinely work in heat. Worth considering if you expand the collection.
