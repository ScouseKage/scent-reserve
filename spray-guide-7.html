Application Rules
  • Never rub after spraying — it breaks down the scent molecules and flattens the drydown.
  • Skin retains fragrance better when moisturised — unscented lotion or Vaseline on pulse points before spraying extends wear significantly.
  • Pulse points (neck, wrists) generate heat which amplifies projection. Neck gives most presence, wrists are more subtle.
  • Fabric holds fragrance longer than skin but prevents the scent from evolving — fine for longevity, not ideal for appreciating a complex fragrance.
  • Reduce by 1 spray if you're indoors in a heated space, it's a warm or hot day, you're in a small enclosed space (car, office, lift), or the occasion calls for subtlety.
  • Add 1 spray if it's cold and you're staying outdoors, you're in a noisy smell environment (bar, restaurant, outdoors in wind), or you're wearing one of the lighter fragrances (162, 318, 346) and want more presence.
  • Hard ceilings that never move — 170, 175, 219, 494, 514: 2 sprays absolute maximum regardless of conditions.
  • When layering a pair, always apply the heavier or base-heavy scent first. Wait 2–3 minutes before applying the second.
